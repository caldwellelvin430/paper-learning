(2) Open-weight models occupy the cost-accuracy Pareto

(3) The capability-cost trade-off is steep. DeepSeek V3.2
delivers 93% of Claude’s capability (56.3% vs. 60.8%) at 11.3×
lower per-case list-price cost.
(4) Systematic failures persist. Missing tool calls dominate
failures (68%), and parameter scale tracks capability only
within our small open-weight sample.
GNA is offered as a recipe as much as a result: swap the tool ex-
ecutors behind the MCP server for your domain’s operations, write
task JSON, and the committed pipeline produces the leaderboard,
Pareto frontier, failure taxonomy, and category breakdown for your
domain. GNA—the MCP tool server, evaluation harness, bench-
mark, and self-hostable API—is publicly available at https://github.
com/gabrielireland/GeoNatureAgent_Benchmark; the dataset and
software are archived at Zenodo [7, 8].
