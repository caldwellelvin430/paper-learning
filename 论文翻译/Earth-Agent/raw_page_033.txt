The Analysis Toolkit provides a suite of statistical and spatiotemporal analysis methods tailored for
remote sensing and geoscience data. Its functionalities include classical time-series trend detection
and decomposition techniques such as linear regression, the Mann–Kendall test, Sen’s slope estima-
tion, and STL decomposition. It also supports change-point detection and seasonal analysis based
on autocorrelation. In addition, the toolkit integrates spatial statistical approaches, including hotspot
direction analysis, as well as methods for spike detection in numerical sequences. A detailed list of
the implemented tools is provided in Table 16.
