The figures below are the artifacts of the original release
of the benchmark (Zhang et al. 2026) and its accompany-
ing report. All benchmark numbers in them were produced
by the original six-dimension judge under the production
scaffold, the L0 configuration whose confound Section 3
(main text) diagnoses. We reproduce them without alteration,
for three reasons. First, completeness: this is the full figure
record, not a curated subset. Second, the flat leaderboards
visible in Figures A10 and A12 are not a historical curios-
ity but the confound in action: a no-LLM rule-based script
sits inside the model pack, and models of very different ca-
pability tie to within a few judge points on T1–T8. Third,
the GRPO figures (Figures A13–A15) document a training
study run against the original L0 judge reward that is not dis-
cussed in the main text; we preserve it because the observed
saturation-at-initialization and reward-variance collapse are
themselves evidence that the L0 reward carries little model-
discriminating signal: a policy can sit at the reward ceiling
before a single gradient step.
